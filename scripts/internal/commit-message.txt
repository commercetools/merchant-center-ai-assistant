Initial release of the AI Assistant for the Merchant Center (commercetools Labs)

An AI chat assistant for the commercetools Merchant Center. It works on a
single commercetools project, is deployed only with commercetools Connect,
uses Gemini through Vertex AI and keeps its state in Custom Objects.

This project is freely available, provided AS IS and UNSUPPORTED. It is
licensed under the MIT License (see LICENSE).

Start with README.md and docs/deployment-runbook.md. The folders plans/ and
openspec/ hold the design documentation of the project.

Parts: service/ (Connect service application), mc-app/ and composed-apps/
(Merchant Center custom applications), mc-view-app/ (custom view).
